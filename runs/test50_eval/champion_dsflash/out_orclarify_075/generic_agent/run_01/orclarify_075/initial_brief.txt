Each unit of product B produced generates 2 units of by-product C at no additional cost.

Any unsold by-product C must be disposed of at a cost of 2 yuan per unit.

Profit per unit: Product A sold yields 4 yuan, Product B sold yields 10 yuan, By-product C sold yields 3 yuan.
