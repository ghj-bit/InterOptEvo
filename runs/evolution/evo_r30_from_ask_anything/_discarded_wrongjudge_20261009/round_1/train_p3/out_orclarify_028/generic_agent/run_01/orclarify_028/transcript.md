## Turn 1 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: What is the annual profit rate for project (1) and in which years can it be invested?

## Turn 1 - User Simulator

The annual profit rate for project (1) is 20% of the investment amount. You can invest in project (1) at the beginning of any of the three years.

## Turn 2 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (1), is the 20% annual profit paid at the end of each year, and does the principal remain available for reinvestment in subsequent years?

## Turn 2 - User Simulator

Yes, the 20% annual profit is paid at the end of each year, and the entire amount—both the original investment and the 20% profit—can be reinvested in any project the next year.
