## Turn 1 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: Are all 125 empty containers from the warehouses required to be shipped to the ports, or is it acceptable to leave some containers at the warehouses if the total port demand (114) is less than the total warehouse inventory

## Turn 1 - User Simulator

It is acceptable to leave some containers at the warehouses. The total port demand is 114 containers, so only 114 of the 125 available containers need to be shipped.
