## Turn 1 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (1), is the 20% annual profit paid out each year and added to the available cash for reinvestment, or is it retained within the project and only realized at the end of the three-year horizon?

## Turn 1 - User Simulator

The 20% annual profit is paid out each year and added to the available cash for reinvestment. You can reinvest the entire amount—both your original investment and the 20% profit—in any project the following year.
