## Turn 1 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: Is the 20% annual profit for project (1) paid out at the end of each year and added to the available cash for reinvestment, or is it compounded within the project so that the principal remains locked until a later date?

## Turn 1 - User Simulator

The 20% annual profit is paid out at the end of each year. The full amount—your original investment plus the 20% profit—becomes available cash that you can reinvest in any project the following year.
