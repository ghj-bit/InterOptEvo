## Turn 1 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (1), is the 20% annual profit paid every year for the full three-year period, or is it paid only in the third year?

## Turn 1 - User Simulator

The 20% profit is paid annually. If you invest in project (1) at the beginning of a year, you receive the 20% profit at the end of that same year. This applies to whichever year you choose to invest in project (1).

## Turn 2 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (1), is the original investment amount returned to you at the end of the third year, or is the 20% annual profit the only cash flow you receive from it?

## Turn 2 - User Simulator

The original investment amount is returned to you along with the 20% annual profit at the end of each year. You receive both the principal and the profit, and the entire amount can be reinvested in the following year.
