## Turn 1 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (1), is the 20% annual profit paid at the end of each year with the principal returned at the end of year 3, and can you invest in project (1) at the beginning of each of the three years?

## Turn 1 - User Simulator

You can invest in project (1) at the beginning of any of the three years. The 20% profit is earned annually, and the entire amount—both your original investment and the 20% profit—can be reinvested in any project the next year.

## Turn 2 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (2), is the 150% total return paid all at once at the end of year 3 (meaning you get back 1.5 times your investment at that time)?

## Turn 2 - User Simulator

No, that is not correct. The investment in project (2) is recovered at the end of the second year, not the end of year 3.

## Turn 3 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: When project (2) is recovered at the end of year 2, do you receive back 150% of the amount you invested (i.e., principal plus 50% profit), or do you receive back only the original principal?

## Turn 3 - User Simulator

You receive back 150% of the amount you invested.

## Turn 4 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (3), is the 160% total return paid all at once at the end of year 3, and can you invest in project (3) at the beginning of any of the three years?

## Turn 4 - User Simulator

No, you can only invest in project (3) at the beginning of the second year. The 160% total return is paid at the end of the third year.

## Turn 5 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (4), is the 40% profit paid at the end of year 3 along with the return of the original principal, and can you invest in project (4) at the beginning of any of the three years?

## Turn 5 - User Simulator

You can only invest in project (4) at the beginning of the third year. The 40% profit is paid at the end of the third year along with the return of the original principal.
