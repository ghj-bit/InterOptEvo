## Turn 1 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (1), is the 20% annual profit paid each year for all three years (i.e., you keep the principal and receive 20% of it as interest at the end of each of years 1, 2, and 3)?

## Turn 1 - User Simulator

No. The 20% is an annual return on whatever amount you invest in project (1) in a given year. You can invest in project (1) at the beginning of any of the three years, and at the end of that year you get back your principal plus the 20% profit. That full amount (principal plus profit) can then be reinvested in any project the following year. It is not a single three-year commitment that pays 20% each year on the original principal.
