## Turn 1 - Generic Agent

QUESTION: For project (1), is the 20% annual profit paid every year for as long as money stays invested, and is the original principal returned at the end of the third year?

## Turn 1 - User Simulator

The 20% profit is earned each year on the amount invested in project (1). At the end of each year, the full amount—your original investment plus that year’s 20% profit—can be reinvested in any project the following year. So the principal is not held back until the end of the third year; it is available for reinvestment every year.
